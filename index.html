Savage Prog Tracker

Raid-night wipes, phase notes and week-over-week progress — right in your browser. Nothing leaves your machine.

How this works (30 seconds)
  • Pick or create a party, fill in the roster with roles.
  • Add an encounter (like M1S). After each pull, log the boss HP % you left it at — that keeps wipe counts and your best-percent record up to date.
  • For DPS checks, set a kill line (“boss must be at or below X% by the check”) and the log will mark each pull pass/fail against it.
  • Write phase notes as you learn, and save strat drafts as versions you can fall back to.
  • The weekly recap builds a short, paste-ready summary for Free Company chat, comparing this week with last week using pull timestamps.
  • Everything is stored in your browser's localStorage. Use Export JSON to back up or move it to another device.
